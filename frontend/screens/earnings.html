How the momentum score works 40 / 25 / 25 / 10

A signed score from −100 to +100: 40% ten-session momentum + 25% three-session momentum + 25% outperformance vs SPY + 10% directional consistency.

Returns use close-to-close compounding. Return factors are divided by 2 × daily volatility × √sessions and capped at ±1. Volatility uses 20 daily returns, with a 0.50% floor. Consistency weights the latest three sessions twice.

Direction changes reduce the score's magnitude by up to 30%. Earnings proximity adds no points. The model requires 21 valid stock closes and 11 matching SPY closes. Today's session becomes eligible at 4:05 PM ET; partial intraday sessions are excluded.

RS is the stock's ten-session return minus SPY's return, in percentage points. Stocks missing required US trading dates are unscored. This is a momentum measure, not an earnings-outcome prediction.
